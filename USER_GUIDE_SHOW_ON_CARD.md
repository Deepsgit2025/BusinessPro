# User Guide: "Show on Card" Toggle Feature

## What Is This Feature?

The "Show on Card" toggle allows you to control which information appears on your business visiting card. You can choose to show or hide:
- **GSTIN** (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number)
- **Business Type** (e.g., Retailer, Wholesaler)
- **Business Category** (e.g., Electronics & Electrical)

---

## Where to Find It

1. Open the app and navigate to **Business Profile**
2. Click/Tap the **"Business Details"** tab
3. You'll see the toggle switches labeled **"Show on card"** next to each field

---

## How to Use

### Step 1: Fill in Your Information
First, make sure you've filled in the fields you want to optionally show:
- **Business Type:** Select from the dropdown list
- **Business Category:** Select from the dropdown list
- **GSTIN:** Enter your GSTIN number (format: 22AAAAA0000A1Z5)

### Step 2: Toggle the "Show on Card" Switch
Next to each field, you'll see a toggle switch labeled **"Show on card"**

**To SHOW a field on your card:**
- Move the toggle switch to the **ON** position (switch turns blue/colored)
- The field will immediately appear on your card preview above

**To HIDE a field from your card:**
- Move the toggle switch to the **OFF** position (switch turns gray)
- The field will immediately disappear from your card preview above

### Step 3: See Changes Instantly
Changes appear on your card preview **immediately** when you toggle the switch. You don't need to save first to see the preview changes.

### Step 4: Save Your Preferences
When you're happy with your choices:
1. Click the **"Save"** button at the bottom
2. You'll see a confirmation message: "Business profile saved"
3. Your toggle preferences are now saved

---

## What Happens When You Restart the App?

✅ Your toggle preferences are automatically saved and restored when you reopen the app.

If you had toggled "Business Type" ON before closing the app, it will still be ON when you reopen the app.

---

## Sharing Your Card

When you click **"Share Card"** button:
- Your card is saved as an image
- **Only the fields you've toggled ON are included** in the shared image
- Fields you've toggled OFF are not visible to people you share with

**Example:**
- You toggle ON: Business Type
- You toggle OFF: Business Category and GSTIN
- When you share the card, the recipient will see the Business Type but NOT the Business Category or GSTIN

---

## Tips & Best Practices

### ✅ Good Practices
1. **Toggle fields ON that you want customers/partners to see**
   - Business Type if it's important to your business identity
   - Business Category if it helps customers understand your business

2. **Save after making changes**
   - Even though you see changes instantly, save ensures they're stored

3. **Test different combinations**
   - Try different toggle combinations to find what looks best for your card

### ❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Don't forget to SAVE**
   - Changes in the preview don't persist unless you click "Save"
   - If you close the app without saving, your toggle changes will be lost

2. **Don't leave fields empty**
   - If you toggle ON a field but don't fill in its value, it won't appear
   - For example: Toggle ON "Business Type" but leave the Business Type dropdown empty → field won't show

3. **Don't confuse toggles with visibility**
   - Toggling a field OFF just hides it - the data is still in the app
   - You can toggle it back ON anytime

## Troubleshooting

### "The toggle doesn't seem to work"
**Solution:**
1. Check that you've filled in the field value (e.g., selected Business Type from dropdown)
2. Make sure you're looking at the preview card at the top of the screen
3. Try toggling it OFF and ON again

### "My changes disappeared when I reopened the app"
**Solution:**
1. This means you didn't click "Save" before closing the app
2. Make your changes again
3. Click "Save" button
4. Now your changes will persist

### "The field still doesn't appear after toggling ON and saving"
**Solution:**
1. Go back to the field (e.g., Business Type dropdown)
2. Make sure you've actually selected a value (not left it empty)
3. Check the toggle is in the ON position
4. Click "Save"

### "My shared card looks different from the preview"
**Solution:**
This might happen if:
1. Your toggles were changed since the last save
2. Make sure all fields you want to share are toggled ON
3. Save your changes
4. Share the card again
